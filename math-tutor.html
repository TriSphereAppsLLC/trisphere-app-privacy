Math Tutor: Step-by-Step

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 25, 2026

On this page

  • Learner records
  • Photo, handwriting and voice
  • Worksheets and Google Play purchases
  • Support and privacy requests

Learner records

You can use Math Tutor without a TriSphere account. Local profile names, goals, math problems and study records are stored on your device. Use the app’s profile and history controls to manage your records. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records.

Photo, handwriting and voice

Photo, handwriting and speech recognition use models bundled with the Android app. The app reads chosen images and writing on your device, and only uses the microphone while you use voice entry. Math Tutor does not send your photos, writing or speech to an online recognition service.

The app can use a photo you select in Android or a photo you take with your camera app. Its temporary camera copy is cleared after the scan flow. You can review and correct the recognized text before solving.

Worksheets and Google Play purchases

Practice pages and worked answer keys go to the print service or PDF destination you choose. Copies saved outside the app are managed separately.

Before purchase options are shown, the app checks a birth year locally, and asks whether the birthday has passed if the person turns 18 that year. The answer is discarded.

Google Play handles payments for the optional Printable Practice Packs purchase. Math Tutor checks the signed purchase record, remembers access on your device and acknowledges purchases through Google Play. Eligible purchases can be restored with the Google Play account used to buy them. Play may receive technical billing events and diagnostics to operate or troubleshoot payments under the Google Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact TriSphere Support, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the details you choose to send. Support records may be kept while we resolve your request and for any applicable recordkeeping obligations. TriSphere cannot remotely retrieve records held only on your device.
