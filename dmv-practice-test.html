Your study records

Your state, answers, coursework, saved exam, flashcard schedule, driving simulator results and study progress are stored on your device. The practice engine responds to your on-screen controls. The app has no publisher account and does not request location, camera or microphone access.

Supervised driving journal

Your optional journal stores dates, driving minutes, skills, driving conditions and any supervisor initials or notes you enter. Entries stay on your device. You can edit or delete a drive. When you save or share a CSV file, the destination you choose receives a copy. Removing the original drive does not remove copies you exported.

Purchases and diagnostics

Google Play handles the optional one-time Complete Study Pass purchase. The app asks Play for your offer and purchase status, checks the signed purchase record, and keeps it on the device to remember access. TriSphere does not receive your payment card details.

Google Play Billing can send technical billing events and diagnostics to Google to operate and troubleshoot purchases. TriSphere Apps LLC does not run a separate app analytics or advertising service.

You can restore eligible purchases using the original Google Play account. Google retains and manages its payment records under the Google Privacy Policy. See Google Play Help for purchase and account support.

Deletion and links

Clearing storage in Android Settings or uninstalling removes the app’s local records and cached purchase receipt. Google Play’s records and any exported files are handled separately.

Opening a handbook or support link takes you to a separate website. The official licensing agency manages your handbook visit under its own policy.

Contact and privacy requests

Contact TriSphere support for privacy questions or requests about records you send to us. We receive the details you choose to submit, so please share only the information we need to help.

Support records may be kept while we resolve your request and for any applicable recordkeeping obligations. The publisher cannot remotely retrieve records held only on your device.
