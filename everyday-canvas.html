Everyday Canvas

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 26, 2026

On this page

Workspace and imported files

Everyday Canvas for Android is provided by TriSphere Apps LLC. Canvases, notes, inbox text, tasks, flashcards, drawings, and other workspace content you enter or share into the app are saved in private storage on your device. When you choose or share an image or PDF, the app reads it and saves a private copy for the workspace. The original file remains with its source.

Everyday Canvas has no TriSphere account or cloud sync. TriSphere does not receive your workspace content through the app. The app has no advertising or developer analytics service.

Task reminders

If you set a task reminder and allow notifications, the app schedules it on your device. An Android notification can display the task text and canvas title. The app also uses Android’s boot and time-change events to reschedule saved reminders. Reminder details are part of the local workspace.

Exports and other services

When you export a canvas, you choose the file destination through Android. The destination or storage provider receives the exported copy and handles it under its own privacy practices. Everyday Canvas does not automatically upload your workspace to TriSphere. Removing a canvas in the app does not remove files you exported elsewhere.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time Local Pro purchase. Everyday Canvas asks Play for available offers and purchase status, verifies the signed purchase record, and saves a copy of that record on your device to remember access. You can restore an eligible purchase through the Google Play account used to buy it. TriSphere does not receive your payment card details.

Google Play may receive purchase information and technical billing diagnostics to process and troubleshoot transactions. Google manages its payment and account records under the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

Android private app storage holds the workspace, imported file copies, settings and cached purchase record. The app disables Android backup of its private data. You can remove individual canvases and inbox items in the app. Deleting a canvas removes its workspace record; its imported file copies may remain in private app storage until you clear app storage or uninstall. Clearing Everyday Canvas storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app removes its private local data, including imported copies and the cached purchase record. Exported files, source files you imported, and Google Play purchase records are managed separately.

Support and privacy requests

Opening TriSphere Support takes you to a separate website. The app does not attach your workspace or purchase record. If you contact us, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the contact details and message you choose to submit and uses them to respond. Support records may be kept while resolving your request and for applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Use the support form for privacy questions or deletion requests about information you sent to us. TriSphere cannot remotely retrieve or delete workspace data stored only on your device.
