Gem Genius: Jewelry Guide

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 27, 2026

On this page

  • Your collection
  • Photos and color comparison
  • Purchases
  • External resources
  • Support and requests

Your collection stays on your device

Gem Genius does not require a TriSphere account. Saved jewelry pieces, folders, budgets, notes and learning progress are kept in the app on your Android device. The app does not synchronize this information to a TriSphere server. You can edit or delete saved pieces in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records unless you have saved your own export.

Photos and color comparison

You can choose a photo through Android’s photo picker or take one with a camera app. Gem Genius uses the chosen image on your device to sample colors and compare them with reference photographs packaged in the app. It keeps temporary previews in the app’s private cache while you work; starting over removes that session’s temporary copy. Gem Genius does not upload these photos for color comparison. A color match is an educational reference, not authentication or a professional appraisal.

Google Play purchases

Optional paid features, when available, use Google Play Billing. Google handles checkout and payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. The app checks the purchase record supplied by Google Play to grant and restore the corresponding feature on this device. A purchase does not transfer your locally stored collection to another device. Google Play may process purchase and diagnostic information needed to operate its service.

External resources

The Buying Resources guide contains links to independent gemology and consumer-education websites. These sites open outside Gem Genius and have their own privacy practices. Opening a link is your choice; the resource directory itself does not send your vault, budget or photo to those sites.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact TriSphere Support, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the details you choose to send and may keep them while resolving your request and meeting applicable recordkeeping obligations. We cannot remotely retrieve records stored only on your device. For questions about this policy or app data, contact support through the website.
