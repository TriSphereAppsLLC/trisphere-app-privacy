Using the app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Traffic Cameras Globe as a personal road-camera explorer for Android. You may use the app and its included materials in accordance with the Google Play Terms and the source operators’ conditions. The app’s design and code remain TriSphere’s property. Roadside images and underlying camera data remain with Caltrans, DriveBC, Fintraffic/Digitraffic, Québec 511 or their respective rights holders. The app is not an official service of, or endorsed by, those operators.

Camera information and travel decisions

California, British Columbia and Finland cameras open operator-published snapshots inside the app. Québec 511 entries open an official camera page in your browser. Images are periodic snapshots, not continuous live video. They can be delayed, unavailable or unsuitable for judging current road safety. Camera coverage is limited to the regions actually listed in the app and may change as operators update their catalogs. Check official road and emergency guidance before making travel or safety decisions.

Plus purchase and saved boards

Camera viewing and search are available without a purchase. The free Morning Commute board holds up to three saved cameras. An optional one-time Google Play Plus purchase unlocks multiple named boards, more saved cameras, custom ordering and two-camera snapshot comparison. The current local price and benefits appear before checkout. There is no Plus subscription. Google Play handles payment and applicable refunds. You can use Restore purchase with the same Google Play account to check an eligible entitlement. Boards are stored locally; restoring Plus does not sync boards from another device.

Privacy

Our Android privacy policy explains direct requests to camera operators, local board storage and Google Play Billing.

Support and changes
