Overview

Forgewood is a village-building game provided by TriSphere Apps LLC. The Android app does not require a TriSphere account and does not contain advertising or a developer analytics SDK. Its town models, gameplay rules and sound are packaged with the app.

Village progress on your device

Construction, inventory, earned Gems, residents, animals, orders, quests, settings and purchase entitlements are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. TriSphere does not receive these game records. Android backup is disabled, and this release has no account or cloud-save service. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local progress and any earned Gem balance; reinstalling does not restore the village.

Google Play purchases

The app offers optional one-time storage upgrades through Google Play. Google shows the local price and handles payment. The app receives the product ID, purchase state, signed purchase data and purchase token needed to verify and apply an upgrade. It checks the signed purchase locally, records the permanent entitlement in the device save, acknowledges eligible purchases through Google Play, and can check the original Google Play account when you tap Restore. TriSphere does not receive your payment-card number, purchase token or village save through its own server.

Google Play may process payment and technical app, device and network information to operate and troubleshoot purchases. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy. This release does not offer new purchases of consumable Gems because local Gem balances cannot be recovered after uninstall.

Your controls and retention

You can clear local game records through Android Settings or by uninstalling. Permanent storage purchases remain tied to the Google Play account used to buy them and can be restored in the Market after reinstalling, subject to Google Play’s current purchase status. Restoring a purchase does not restore your village save. Google manages its own transaction records and refund process.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or support, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. If you choose to contact us, we receive the details you send and use them to respond. We will update this page before materially changing the app’s data practices. See the Forgewood Android terms.
