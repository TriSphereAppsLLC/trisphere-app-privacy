Using the guide

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Gem Genius as a gemstone and jewelry learning and record-keeping app for Android. Its profiles, examples and photo color comparisons are educational references. They do not verify authenticity, grade a stone, or provide a certified appraisal. Consult a qualified gemologist or laboratory for decisions that require authentication or valuation. Use the app and its included materials in accordance with the Google Play Terms.

Your records

Your original collection notes and chosen photos remain yours. Local records stay on your device; Gem Genius does not offer a TriSphere cloud backup or transfer purchases into a copy of your data. Keep a separate copy of any export you need to preserve. The app’s design, reference text and included photographs remain the property of TriSphere or their respective credited owners.

Optional purchases

Gem Genius may offer separate Google Play purchases for Premium and Pro Tools. Each purchase unlocks only the features named in its in-app offer; one entitlement does not automatically include the other. The current price, billing period and included features appear before checkout. Google Play handles payment, subscription management and applicable refunds. Use Restore purchases in the app to check active entitlements for the Google Play account used to buy them. Restoring a purchase does not restore local collection records.

Privacy

The Gem Genius privacy policy explains local photos and collection data, Google Play purchases, external links and support messages.

Support and changes
