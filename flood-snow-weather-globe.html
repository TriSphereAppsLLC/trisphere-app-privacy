Overview

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Flood & Snow Weather Globe. It opens on a preset New York forecast. You can explore other places without creating an account or granting GPS access. The app has no ads, public profiles, or background location tracking.

Place searches

When you type a city or address, the app gives that text and your language preference to Android’s place-finding service. You may also enter coordinates or choose a map pin. A pin may identify a precise location, including a home if you select it. Availability and handling by the device’s place-finding service vary. See Android Geocoder.

Weather and map requests

For a selected forecast, MET Norway receives coordinates rounded to two decimal places. For selected radar, snowpack, or alert views, NOAA and the U.S. National Weather Service receive a point or map area. NASA receives the imagery layer, region, and observation date or time for imagery you view. OpenStreetMap receives requests for map tiles at the area and zoom level shown. Direct requests also reveal network information such as your IP address. Providers may retain technical logs under their own policies.

Saved places and settings

Favorite names, selected place, map layer, units, clock settings, recent forecast responses, snow-depth readings, and map cache data stay on this device. A name you give a favorite is not sent to TriSphere. These local records help the app show your chosen places and load recent views faster.

Google Play subscriptions

Google Play handles optional Plus payments, renewals, and cancellation. When a subscription is checked, the app sends its Google Play purchase token over HTTPS to TriSphere’s verification service, which checks with Google Play whether the benefits are active and for how long. TriSphere does not receive your payment-card number. Google’s billing component may send billing and technical app, device, and network data to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy. A temporary reviewer preview, if shown, is separate from a paid purchase and does not grant lasting entitlement.

Your control and retention

To remove a favorite, open Saved places, edit the place, and choose Remove saved place. Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling removes local records. Weather and map providers manage their own request logs. You can manage or cancel a Plus subscription in Google Play.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. If you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send and use them to respond. We will update this page if the app’s data practices materially change. See also the Android app terms.
