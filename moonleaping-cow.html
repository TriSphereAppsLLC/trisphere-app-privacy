MoonLeaping: Cow Jump Game privacy policy

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 27, 2026

On this page

  • Local game progress
  • Permissions and connected services
  • Purchases and restore
  • Support and deletion

Local game progress

The game saves your chosen world, character, trail, earned coin record, mission progress and sound, weather and movement settings in private storage on your device. You can play without a TriSphere account. Android backup is disabled for this app; clearing app data or uninstalling removes these local records.

Permissions and connected services

The game does not request camera, microphone or location permission. It has no advertising SDK or publisher analytics service. Artwork, audio and the four worlds are packaged with the app. Optional Google Play purchases use Google’s billing service.

Purchases and restore

Google Play supplies product prices and purchase status for optional cosmetic characters and trails. The app checks the signed purchase record, keeps eligible ownership on the device and acknowledges valid orders through Google Play. Google may process device, account, purchase and billing diagnostic information to operate transactions under the Google Privacy Policy. TriSphere does not receive payment card details. Use Restore purchases in the Moon Shop with the original Google Play account to recover eligible items.

Support and deletion

If you contact TriSphere Support, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the details you choose to send. Please leave out a child’s identifying details unless needed to resolve a request. The support service may keep that message while resolving it and for applicable recordkeeping obligations. TriSphere cannot remotely retrieve game records held only on your device. Google Play separately manages its transaction history.
