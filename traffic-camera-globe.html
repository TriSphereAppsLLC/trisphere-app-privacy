Overview

Traffic Camera Globe is provided by TriSphere Apps LLC. It helps you browse public roadside camera locations, open a selected operator image, and save cameras to boards. The app does not require an account or location permission and contains no advertising or developer analytics SDK.

Camera requests

The app includes a catalog of camera locations. A camera image loads only after you open that camera. Your device requests the image directly from the named operator, currently Caltrans or Fintraffic/Digitraffic. The operator can receive your IP address and standard connection information. The app shows the operator and a source link next to the image. Each operator handles its own requests under its privacy terms.

Saved boards and settings

Your saved cameras and reduced-motion preference stay in private storage on your device. TriSphere does not receive them. Android backup is disabled for this app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes those local entries. Restoring a purchase does not restore your boards.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time Plus purchase and supplies the app with its current purchase state. The app checks Play ownership when it opens or resumes and when you tap Restore, then acknowledges an eligible purchase through Google Play Billing. The app does not send your purchase token or saved boards to TriSphere or its own server. It does not receive your payment-card number. Google manages payment details under the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and control

Saved boards and settings remain on your device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall. The app checks the current Google Play purchase state when opened or restored; it does not keep a separate TriSphere account or purchase-history database. Google Play handles refunds and purchase records. You can clear local data in Android Settings.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. If you choose to contact us, we receive the details you send and use them to respond. We will update this page before adding new camera providers or materially changing data practices.
