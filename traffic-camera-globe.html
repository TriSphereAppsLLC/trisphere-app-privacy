Overview

Traffic Cameras Globe is provided by TriSphere Apps LLC. It helps you browse public roadside camera locations, open selected operator images or official camera-page links, and save cameras to boards. The app does not require an account or location permission and contains no advertising or developer analytics SDK.

Camera requests

The app includes a catalog of camera locations. Operator camera images may load when you view a region preview or saved-board thumbnail, and when you open a camera. Your device requests each image directly from the named source: Caltrans in California, ODOT TripCheck in Oregon (including images supplied by participating camera owners), the Ohio Department of Transportation's OHGO camera service in Ohio, the City of Austin's public camera service in the Austin area of Texas, DriveBC in British Columbia, Fintraffic/Digitraffic in Finland, or Statens vegvesen in Norway. When you choose Play on an eligible California camera, your device requests an HLS video stream directly from Caltrans. The operator can receive the camera image or stream address you requested, your IP address and standard connection information. The camera viewer shows the source and a link. Most images are periodic snapshots; California video is available only where the operator provides it. Each source handles its own requests under its privacy terms.

Québec 511 entries open the official operator video page in your browser only after you tap its link. The app does not retrieve or embed that video. The U.S. state directory includes separate official traffic and traveler links for all 50 states and the District of Columbia. Tapping one opens the operator's website in a browser surface or separate browser app, according to the operator's terms; that website handles the request under its own privacy practices. Searching the on-device catalog does not send your search text to TriSphere.

Road map requests

When you explore the RoadView map, your device requests the visible road-map tiles, labels, and style assets from OpenFreeMap. Map data is contributed through OpenStreetMap and styled with OpenMapTiles data. The app sends no saved boards, purchase state, search text, or account details to OpenFreeMap. As with any internet request, the provider and its delivery network can receive the requested tile address and connection information. OpenFreeMap says its routine access logs are anonymized and do not contain IP addresses; error logs can include IP addresses for up to seven days, and temporary IP logs can be enabled during security incidents. Read the OpenFreeMap Privacy Policy for details. The app does not download map regions in the background.

Saved boards and settings

Your saved cameras and reduced-motion preference are stored in private storage on your device. TriSphere does not receive them. Android cloud backup is disabled, but a device-to-device transfer may copy those records on some devices. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local entries on that device. Restoring a purchase does not sync your boards.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time Plus purchase and supplies the app with its current purchase state. The app checks Play ownership when it opens or resumes and when you tap Restore, then acknowledges an eligible purchase through Google Play Billing. After Play confirms ownership, the app stores only the time of that check on your device so Plus can remain available for up to seven days during a temporary Play outage; a successful Play check without an active purchase clears it. The app does not send your purchase token or saved boards to TriSphere or its own server. It does not receive your payment-card number. Google manages payment details under the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and control

Saved boards and settings remain on your device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall. The app checks the current Google Play purchase state when opened or restored; it does not keep a separate TriSphere account or purchase-history database. Google Play handles refunds and purchase records. You can clear local data in Android Settings.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. If you choose to contact us, we receive the details you send and may retain them while resolving your request and meeting applicable recordkeeping obligations. We will update this page before adding new camera providers or materially changing data practices.
