Tire Tornado 2: Pop & Drive

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 25, 2026

On this page

  • Game saves and settings
  • Purchases and diagnostics
  • Deleting data
  • Privacy requests

Your career and game settings

Your scores, medals, scrap, vehicles, upgrades, completed runs and other career rewards are saved on your device. Music, haptic feedback and reduced motion preferences are also local. You can play without a publisher account. The game has no advertising SDK, publisher analytics service or location feature.

Purchases and diagnostics

Optional vehicles, paint and arenas use Google Play Billing. Google supplies prices and purchase status. The game verifies and privately stores a signed purchase record on the device and acknowledges your purchase through Play. TriSphere does not receive payment card details.

Google Play Billing can send technical billing events and diagnostics to Google to operate and troubleshoot purchases.

In the shop, choose Restore purchases with your original Google Play account to recover eligible purchases. Google’s own transaction records are managed under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data and opening support

Clearing storage or uninstalling the game removes local career data, earned items, settings and cached purchase records. Your Google Play purchases and transaction records are handled separately.

Opening support in your browser does not attach a game save or receipt. Our support site receives only the information you choose to send.

Contact and privacy requests

Contact TriSphere support for privacy questions or requests about records you send to us. We receive the details you choose to submit, so please share only the information we need to help.

Support records may be kept while we resolve your request and for any applicable recordkeeping obligations. The publisher cannot remotely retrieve records held only on your device.
