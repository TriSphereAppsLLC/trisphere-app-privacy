Using the app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Flood & Snow Weather Globe for personal weather exploration on Android. You may use the app and its included content while complying with the Google Play Terms and the terms of the weather and map data providers shown in the app. The app and its presentation remain TriSphere’s property; source maps, imagery, and forecasts remain with their respective providers. Do not redistribute app assets or present provider data as your own.

Weather data and safety

Forecasts are predictions. Radar, satellite, snow, and surface-water layers are observations or modeled analyses that can be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. A surface-water image is not a real-time flood warning or a street-safety determination. Do not rely on the app alone for emergency, travel, property, or other safety decisions; follow your local weather and emergency authority.
