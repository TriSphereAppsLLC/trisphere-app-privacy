Independent study app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Signal Mile as an independent learning app. It is not operated, sponsored or endorsed by a DMV, MVC, BMV or another government agency. Its lessons, simulator, practice questions and scores are not official exam questions or results. State agencies set the current rules and administer actual tests. Use the official USA.gov state motor-vehicle directory to find your licensing agency and verify current requirements.

Learning and safety

The driving simulator is a practice exercise. It cannot replace supervised driving, an approved course when one is required, or instructions from a licensed instructor and your state agency. Follow current traffic laws and your supervising driver when driving a real vehicle. Use the app only while safely parked or as a passenger; do not interact with it while operating a vehicle.

You may use the app and its included materials in accordance with the Google Play Terms. The app and its original design and study materials remain TriSphere’s property. Your own journal entries remain yours.

Complete Study Pass

The free version provides the lessons and limited practice described in the app. Complete Study Pass is an optional one-time Google Play purchase for the additional exams, flashcards and mistake review identified before checkout. Google Play displays the current local price, handles payment and applicable refunds, and supplies the purchase record used by the app. Restore purchases checks for an eligible purchase on the same Google Play account. A restored purchase does not restore study history or journal records from another device.

Your records and privacy

Study progress and optional driving-journal entries are stored locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove them. If you export a journal CSV, keep your own copy; deleting the app does not delete copies you sent elsewhere. The DMV Practice Test privacy policy explains the app’s data and purchase handling in more detail.

Support and changes

For app or purchase-access help, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. We may update these terms as the app changes and will show the updated date here. These terms do not remove rights you have under applicable consumer law.
