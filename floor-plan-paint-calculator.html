Floor Plan & Paint Calculator

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 25, 2026

On this page

  • Your jobs and measurements
  • Optional camera measurement
  • Blueprint and material exports
  • Field Pro purchases
  • Support and privacy requests

Your jobs and measurements

Floor Plan & Paint Calculator stores job names, room dimensions, layouts, paint and flooring estimates and plumbing notes on your device. You can use it without a TriSphere account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local job library and settings.

Optional camera measurement

On supported devices, Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) uses the camera and motion sensors to help place the corners and points you mark. You can use tape measurements without starting a camera session. Only accepted measurements are added to your saved job.

The app does not save camera images or use ARCore’s Cloud Anchor or Geospatial features. Google documents that ARCore collects AR usage and diagnostic data, an account identifier when signed in, or a device identifier otherwise. These technical records are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the ARCore data disclosure.

Blueprint and material exports

When you pick a PDF destination, the app temporarily keeps a private copy of the selected job. This keeps your export from switching to a different job while the file picker is open. The temporary copy is removed when the export is finished or canceled. Starting another export also clears abandoned copies older than seven days.

PDFs and material lists go to the destination you choose. Copies outside the app are managed by that destination or recipient.

Field Pro purchases

Google Play handles the optional one-time Field Pro purchase. The app requests prices and purchase status and keeps a signed record of eligible purchases. You can restore your purchase through the same Google Play account. Technical billing events and diagnostics may go to Google to operate and troubleshoot transactions under the Google Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact TriSphere Support, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the details you choose to send. Support records may be kept while we resolve your request and for any applicable recordkeeping obligations. TriSphere cannot remotely retrieve records held only on your device.
