Using the app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides Everyday Canvas as a personal study, planning, and idea workspace for Android. You may use the app and its included materials in accordance with the Google Play Terms. The app, its design, and included materials remain TriSphere’s property. Your original notes and other content remain yours.

Your work stays on your device

Boards, notes, reminders, flashcards, drawings, and imported files are stored by the app on your device. Everyday Canvas does not provide an account, cloud backup, or synchronization between devices. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove your local work. Export tools let you save supported board formats to a location you choose; please keep your own copies of work you need to preserve.

Local Pro purchase

The free version supports one active canvas with up to ten objects. Local Pro is an optional one-time Google Play purchase that unlocks the paid tools described in the app, including more active canvases and objects, PDF notes, flashcards, drawing, automatic layouts, and formula tables. The current local price and benefits appear before checkout. Google Play handles payment and applicable refunds. You can use Restore purchases in the app to check for an entitlement on the current Google Play account. Restoring Local Pro does not restore boards or imported files from another device.
