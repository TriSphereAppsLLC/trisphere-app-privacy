Using the app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides This is a planning and estimating tool. Verify room dimensions, camera points, paint specifications and recorded pipe routes on site before buying materials or carrying out work. It does not locate hidden pipes or certify a building plan. Use the app in accordance with the Google Play Terms. Do not redistribute the app or its included assets as your own.

Local records

Job names, dimensions, layouts, estimates and notes stay on your device. There is no TriSphere account or cloud backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local jobs. Exports you send to another destination are then managed by that destination.

Optional purchases

The free job supports two rooms on one floor. Optional one-time Field Pro adds a larger job library, more rooms and additional floors. Check the current local price and included benefits before buying. Use Restore purchases with the original Google Play account to recover eligible access. Restore does not recreate deleted jobs. Google Play handles payment, transaction history and applicable refunds.
