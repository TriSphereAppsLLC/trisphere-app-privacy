PhotoFusion Pro

Privacy policy for Android · Updated November 25, 2026

On this page

  • Photos, collages and projects
  • Google Play purchases
  • Storage, security and deletion
  • Support and privacy requests

Photos, collages and projects

PhotoFusion Pro for Android is provided by TriSphere Apps LLC. Select photos and project files through Android’s Photos or Files picker. PhotoFusion processes your photos, facial adjustments, collages and layers on the device. The app stores saved projects, thumbnails and editing settings in its private storage.

Exported files go to the Android destination you choose. The storage provider you select handles that copy under its own policy.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles optional purchases. PhotoFusion receives and checks a signed purchase record, keeps a device copy for restoring Pro and confirms valid transactions through Play. Eligible purchases can be restored using the Google Play account that made the purchase.

Google Play may receive billing events and diagnostics, such as app version, device or network characteristics and payment errors, to operate and troubleshoot payments. TriSphere does not receive payment card numbers. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s handling of this information.

Storage, security and deletion

The Android app has no TriSphere account, advertising or developer analytics. Android private app storage protects saved work and cached purchase records. Google Play billing traffic uses encrypted connections.

Clearing PhotoFusion’s app storage or uninstalling removes local private project files and cached purchase access. Exported copies and the original files chosen from Photos or Files are managed separately. The Google Play account holder can manage purchase records with Google.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact TriSphere Support, TriSphere Apps LLC receives the contact information and details you choose to send. Opening the support page does not attach photos, projects or receipts.

Support records may be kept while resolving your request and for applicable recordkeeping obligations. Use the support form for privacy and deletion requests. TriSphere cannot remotely retrieve project files held only on your device.
