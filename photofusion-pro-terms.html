Using the app

TriSphere Apps LLC provides PhotoFusion Pro as a creative editing app for Android. You may use the app in accordance with the Google Play Terms. You retain your rights to photos and projects you import or create. Only edit or share media you have the right to use. The app's code, interface and supplied design assets remain TriSphere's property.

Editing and exports

The app offers on-device photo adjustments, projects, layers, masks, text, portrait controls and collages. Some tools and export formats require Pro, as shown in the app. Keep a separate copy of important originals and exported work. Projects are stored on the device; uninstalling the app, clearing its data or losing the device may remove local projects.

Live Studio camera sharing

Live Studio starts only when you choose to use the camera. Its optional private Wi-Fi source lets a receiver on your local network view a processed camera frame through a temporary encrypted link. Share the session link and certificate details only with receivers you trust. Stop sharing when you finish; the app closes its source when the session stops or the app leaves the foreground. A receiver or recording program you control may save its own copy, so check that program's settings before you begin.

One-time Pro purchase

The layer workspace and transparent composite PNG export require an optional one-time Pro purchase through Google Play. The app displays the current Play price before checkout. Pro is not a subscription. Google Play handles payment and applicable refunds. Restore purchases checks an eligible entitlement on the same Google Play account. Restoring Pro does not transfer local project files from another device.

Privacy

Our Android privacy policy explains local editing, camera permission, the temporary Wi-Fi source, Google Play Billing and support contact.

Support and changes

For help with an edit or purchase, contact james@trisphereapps.com or use TriSphere Support. We may update these terms as the app changes and will show the updated date here. These terms do not remove rights you have under applicable consumer law.
