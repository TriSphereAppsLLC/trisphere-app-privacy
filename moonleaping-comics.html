Books, drawings and learning records

Children can read, draw and play without a TriSphere account. The bookshop saves pages read, coloring sheets and pencil marks, lantern colors, handwriting practice, passport stamps and game scores in private storage on the device. Drawings and learning records stay on the device. The app has no child profiles, public posts or third-party advertising.

Read Along and pronunciation

Read Along reads the book’s prepared words using an installed English voice that works offline. Children mark their own pronunciation practice. The bookshop does not record voices or access the microphone, camera or location.

Grown-up purchases and billing diagnostics

A grown-up can unlock a world or the complete collection and restore eligible purchases through Google Play. Google handles payment in its own checkout. TriSphere does not receive payment-card details.

Google Play uses the product information and purchase token to check ownership. The app verifies the signed purchase record, saves it privately on the device and acknowledges valid orders with Google Play. Play’s billing tools may process the app version, device or network characteristics and billing errors to operate and troubleshoot purchases.

Google handles the account’s purchase history under the Google Privacy Policy. Grown-ups can manage account purchases through Google Play Help.

Support and other apps

A grown-up can open TriSphere’s website or the listing for its separate MoonLeaping game. The app asks for confirmation before opening a separate browser or store and sends only the destination address. A child’s drawings and reading records stay in the bookshop.

If a grown-up contacts TriSphere through the support form, we receive the contact details, description and any attachment they choose to send. Our support team uses that information to help with the issue. Please leave out a child’s identifying details.

Removing local data and purchase records

Clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app removes local drawings, learning records, scores and cached purchase receipts. Google Play separately manages the grown-up’s order history. A grown-up can restore an eligible paid library using the original Play account.

Contact and privacy requests

Grown-ups can contact TriSphere Apps LLC through TriSphere support or email james@trisphereapps.com with privacy questions or to request deletion of a support record. Support records may be kept while we resolve the request and for applicable recordkeeping obligations.
