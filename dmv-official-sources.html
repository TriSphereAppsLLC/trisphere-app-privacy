Official driver handbook sources

50 states and Washington, DC · Checked against the app’s source list November 27, 2026

Signal Mile is an independent practice app. It is not run, sponsored or endorsed by any licensing agency. The links below open the public handbook or manual page for each state or Washington, DC. Each practice item also offers its own source link inside the app. Agencies can change rules or move pages; confirm current requirements with your state agency before taking the official test.

Use the official USA.gov state motor-vehicle directory to verify the agency for your location.
