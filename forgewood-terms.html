Using the game

Game art, music, software and characters remain the property of TriSphere or their credited creators. Do not redistribute the app or its assets as your own.

Local saves

Forgewood saves the village on your device. This release does not provide a TriSphere account or cloud save. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local village, inventory and earned Gems. A permanent purchase can be restored through Google Play, but that restore does not recreate your village. We recommend keeping the app installed if you want to preserve current progress.

Optional permanent upgrades

The Market may offer Trail Pack (2× material storage), Barn Expansion (5×), and Founder’s Vault (12× material storage plus five listed soundscapes) as one-time purchases. The current price and final benefits appear before purchase in the app and Google Play checkout. Google Play handles payment, purchase records, refunds and purchase status. Use Restore purchases in the Market with the original Google Play account to recover an eligible permanent upgrade. Consumable Gems are not sold in this Android release.
